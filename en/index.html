    Studio

    One person, from the first sketch to handing over the keys.

    dexo is an architecture studio based at Passatge Gaspar de Portolà, 1, in Balaguer (Lleida, Catalonia). Since 2011 we have been designing architecture, renovation and interior projects, and we also take on construction management and health and safety coordination for homes, public buildings and commercial premises.

    More about the studio

    Every commission starts from the site and the real conditions of the work. We look for a specific answer for each project: functional, well-built spaces that are easy to understand, from the first idea to the last detail.

    We support our clients through every stage, from planning and design to permits, construction and supervision. Having a single point of contact means close follow-up and quick decisions.

    We build sustainability into every project to reduce environmental impact and improve the energy efficiency of buildings, always working closely with the client and their goals.
